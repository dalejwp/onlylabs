## 5. The Multi-Step Form (The Conversion Weapon)

Every funnel uses a 5 to 7 step form. Here is the psychology:

1. **Step 1: Low-commitment question.** "What are you looking to achieve?" Buttons only. No typing.
2. **Step 2: Qualifying micro-commitment.** Slider or dropdown. Builds momentum.
3. **Step 3: Specificity.** Turnover, headcount, or funding amount.
4. **Step 4: Urgency.** Timeline question. Flags hot leads.
5. **Step 5: Name and work email.** Never phone first.
6. **Step 6: Phone and company.** Delivered with a benefit statement.
7. **Step 7: Confirmation plus AI-generated summary** of what they will receive.

Conversion lift over single-page forms: 2.4x to 3.1x based on industry data.
