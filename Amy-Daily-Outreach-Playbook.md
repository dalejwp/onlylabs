## PART 2 — The 3 things you're trying to spot

Before we get into the daily steps, you need to know **who is a buyer** so you don't waste time on the wrong people.

A good lead has **all three** of these:

| What | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **UK-based company** | Their LinkedIn header says London, Manchester, Bristol, Edinburgh, Leeds, Birmingham, etc. NOT US, NOT India, NOT Dubai. |
| **They're hiring or scaling** | Their company has 11–200 employees on LinkedIn, AND they've recently posted about hiring, growth, or struggling with talent. |
| **The person is senior** | Their job title is one of: Founder, CEO, COO, Head of People, Head of Talent, Talent Acquisition Manager, CTO, Engineering Manager. |

If they tick all three → 💚 add them.
If they only tick two → 🟡 skip them, plenty more out there.
If they tick one or zero → 🔴 hard skip.

## PART 3 — Your daily LinkedIn routine (45 minutes)

Do this Monday to Friday. Skip weekends.

### Step 3.1 — Open the saved search (1 minute)

Click this exact link and bookmark it: 📋

```
https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/people/?keywords=hiring%20developers&geoUrn=%5B%22101165590%22%5D&network=%5B%22S%22%2C%22O%22%5D&origin=FACETED_SEARCH&titleFreeText=Head%20of%20Talent
```

That URL is pre-filtered to: UK only · 2nd or 3rd-degree connections · job title contains "Head of Talent". You can change the title at the top to search "Founder", "COO", etc. throughout the week.

### Step 3.2 — Send 20 connection requests (10 minutes)

Scroll down the results. For each person who fits the 3 buyer rules:

1. Click their name to open their profile.
2. Quickly check: do they tick all 3 boxes? (UK · 11–200 staff · senior title)
3. If yes → click the blue **"Connect"** button.
4. **DO NOT click "Add a note"** — sending an empty connection request gets accepted 2–3 times more often. Just click "Send."

Do this 20 times. That's it for this step.

💭 If LinkedIn says "we've sent the limit of weekly invitations" — stop. You've done enough. Come back tomorrow.

### Step 3.3 — Check your Notifications for new accepts (2 minutes)

Click the 🔔 bell icon at the top. Look for "X accepted your invitation."

For each new accept, **open their profile** and write down in the spreadsheet: 📋

- **Date** = today
- **Channel** = LinkedIn
- **Name** = Their full name
- **Company** = Their company name
- **Stage** = "Accepted — needs DM"
- **Next action** = "DM tomorrow"

Don't message them today. Wait 2 days. It feels less salesy.

### Step 3.4 — Send DMs to people who accepted 2-3 days ago (20 minutes)

Open the spreadsheet. Filter to rows where **Stage = "Accepted — needs DM"** AND the **Date was 2-3 days ago**.

For each one, open their LinkedIn profile and look at:
- What their company does (read their "About")
- What their job title is
- What they've posted about recently (scroll their profile)

Now pick the DM template below that fits **best**. You'll change just 4–5 words to make it personal — that's it.

```

Happy to send the side-by-side cost breakdown for the role you're hiring if useful — no pitch deck, no call.

Dale

```

```

Quick reason for reaching out — we help UK companies hire South African staff at about half the cost of UK equivalents (same English, 1-hour timezone, no SA entity needed). If [COMPANY] has any hires planned, happy to send a side-by-side cost breakdown. No call, no pitch deck — just the numbers.
```

### 💭 The personalisation rule

For every DM you send, **change at least these 3 things**:
1. **[FIRST NAME]** — their actual first name. Never "Hi there" or "Hi friend."
2. **[COMPANY]** — their company's name. NEVER leave this as a placeholder. Read the profile.
3. **One specific detail** — what their company does (in the first line) OR what they posted about recently.

If you're not sure what the company does → read the first sentence of their LinkedIn "About" section. It tells you exactly.

After sending each DM, update the spreadsheet row:
- **Stage** = "DM sent"
- **Next action** = "Wait for reply, follow up in 5 days"
- **Notes** = (Anything you noticed, e.g. "Series A SaaS, hiring 3 devs")

## PART 7 — What NOT to do (very important)

- ❌ **NEVER** message someone who works for a competitor of SA Desk. If their LinkedIn says "EOR", "Employer of Record", "Deel", "Remote.com", "Oyster", "Multiplier" — SKIP them.
- ❌ **NEVER** promise a specific price. Always say "around £30,000–£40,000 saved per hire" — never "we'll save you £45,000."
- ❌ **NEVER** argue back. If someone is rude, just send the Type 2 polite-no reply and move on.
- ❌ **NEVER** copy and paste a DM without changing the name and company. People can tell.
- ❌ **NEVER** include a link to sadesk.co.uk in the first DM. Links kill reply rates. Drive the conversation, then share the link in a follow-up.
- ❌ **NEVER** send more than 25 connection requests in one day or LinkedIn will flag the account.
